Privacy Policy for Encil

Effective Date: October 23, 2026
Developer/Entity: Kapil Gupta
Application Name: Encil (Pledge Management System)

1. Introduction

Welcome to Encil ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal and financial information. This Privacy Policy is designed to inform you about how we handle your data when you use our offline-first Android application, Encil (designed specifically for pledge management).

By downloading, installing, or using Encil, you acknowledge and agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please refrain from using the application.

2. Our Core Commitment: Offline-First Architecture

Encil is built with an offline-first design philosophy. This means:

  • Local Data Storage: All your core operational data—including pledge records, borrower names, addresses, principal amounts, interest calculations, and activity logs—is stored exclusively in a secure, encrypted local database (Room Database) directly on your Android device.
  • No Centralized Server: We do not operate external remote servers to collect, store, or process your financial business records. Your business data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export or sync it.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

Because Encil functions primarily offline:

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your customers' personal identifiable information (PII) to any third-party servers.
  • We do not track your financial transactions, loan amounts, or profit margins.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize any user data.

4. Permissions and Third-Party Integrations

While Encil operates offline for its daily functions, it integrates with specific device features and optional cloud services to enhance your productivity:

A. Biometric Authentication
Purpose: To provide quick and secure access to the app using your device’s fingerprint or facial recognition hardware.
Data Handling: Biometric credentials are processed entirely within your device's secure hardware enclave (Android Keystore). Encil never reads, captures, or stores your biometric data.

B. Storage and File Provider (FileProvider)
Purpose: To generate and share exported reports (in PDF, Excel, or JSON formats) and application backups.
Data Handling: When you choose to export data or share files via WhatsApp, Email, or save them to your device storage, the app utilizes Android’s secure FileProvider mechanism to package files safely.

C. Optional Google Drive Integration (Cloud Backup & Restore)
Purpose: If you choose to enable cloud backup or restore features, Encil requests access to your personal Google Drive via Google OAuth API.
Data Handling: The app only accesses or writes backup files created explicitly by Encil within your personal Google Drive storage container. We do not have access to any other files in your Google Drive.

5. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security protocols to protect your local data:

  • Password & PIN Protection: Encil utilizes encrypted hashing for passwords and secure PIN locks.
  • Session Security: Automated logout mechanisms and failed-attempt safeguards.

6. Children's Privacy

Encil is a professional business and financial record-keeping tool intended for use by adult business owners and merchants. It is not directed at children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by revising the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
